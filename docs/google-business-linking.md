# Google Business Profile workflow

Shubh Estate Brokers uses one verified Google Business Profile for the brokerage office. Individual properties are promoted through Google Business Profile posts that link back to their dedicated listing pages on https://shubhestatebroker.in.

## Recommended property post format

- Use the actual unit/project image that is approved for marketing.
- Headline: project + sector + configuration + strongest unit USP.
- Include area, floor, furnishing/view and asking price when approved for public marketing.
- Add a short urgency or availability message only when accurate.
- Use the Google Business Profile post action button to link directly to the property's canonical page on the website.
- Do not create separate Google Business Profiles for individual flats or resale units.

## Website connection

The website links back to the verified Google Business Profile from the footer, contact page and founder/about page. The RealEstateAgent JSON-LD includes the Google Business Profile as a sameAs/hasMap reference and uses the same office identity and public phone numbers.
