# Founder advisory positioning

Shubh Estate Brokers is positioned as a founder-led Gurugram real estate advisory firm rather than a conventional inventory-led brokerage.

Founder Arun Madan's former senior-level banking exposure is highlighted around mortgage and credit understanding, property valuation, title creation and title assessment, banking documentation, due diligence, investment safety and risk assessment.

Core brand principle: integrity before commission, with fair and transparent advice and willingness to advise a client not to proceed when price, title, documentation, financing or investment risk does not stand up to scrutiny.
