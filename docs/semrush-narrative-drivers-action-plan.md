# Semrush Narrative Drivers action plan

Baseline reviewed: 10 August 2026, ChatGPT narrative-driver screenshots comparing Shubh Estate Brokers with 99acres, MagicBricks, NoBroker and Housing.com.

## Finding

Shubh Estate Brokers was absent from the visible non-branded ChatGPT answers. Competitors and large portals appeared for buyer guidance, price transparency, home-loan coordination, NRI documentation, property selling and trust-related questions. This is primarily a share-of-voice and evidence gap rather than a negative-sentiment problem.

## Prompts intentionally not targeted

The report also showed questions about industrial units, warehouses, corporate offices, rental management, periodic inspections, tenant screening and tax optimisation. These should not become website claims or SEO pages unless Shubh Estate Brokers confirms that it actively provides the service and can support the claim with genuine experience.

## Off-site evidence programme

1. Request two or three genuine reviews per week from clients after a real service milestone.
2. Ask the client to describe the actual process—shortlisting, price explanation, documentation, loan coordination, NRI support or seller communication—in their own words.
3. Never provide a pre-written positive review, offer an incentive or create a review for the client.
4. Respond to every review factually and without adding private transaction details.
5. Keep the business name, office address, phone numbers, website and service description consistent on the Google Business Profile and legitimate real-estate or local-business profiles.
6. Publish anonymised case studies only when the underlying transaction is genuine and the client/property information is approved for use.
7. Review Semrush Narrative Drivers monthly for non-branded questions where Shubh remains absent, then update the most relevant existing page before creating another URL.

## Measurement

Track the following monthly:

- Share of voice and presence for the priority non-branded questions.
- Cited Shubh Estate Brokers pages and external sources.
- Organic landing-page impressions and clicks in Search Console.
- GA4 calls, WhatsApp clicks, enquiry submissions and seller submissions from the narrative pages.
- Genuine Google review volume, themes and response rate.

AI mentions cannot be guaranteed. The operating goal is to publish clear, verifiable and consistent evidence that search engines, AI systems and prospective clients can evaluate.
