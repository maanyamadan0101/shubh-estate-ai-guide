### Profile quality checklist

- Search for an existing listing before creating a new one.
- Use one canonical website URL and the exact business name.
- Match the office address, phone numbers and opening hours across platforms.
- Add real office, founder and team photos; do not reuse stock images as evidence of the location.
- Choose the closest accurate categories, not every available category.
- Write for customers; do not repeat “property dealer in Gurgaon” or similar phrases unnaturally.
- Save login owner, verification status, public profile URL and last-reviewed date in an internal tracker.
- Ask only genuine clients for honest reviews; never buy, gate or script reviews.

## Do not do

- Do not buy links, rent links or pay for followed advertorial links.
- Do not use private blog networks, automated directory tools or mass guest-post marketplaces.
- Do not exchange links at scale or require keyword-rich anchor text.
- Do not create fake offices, practitioner profiles, awards, reviews or RERA claims.
- Do not publish near-duplicate city or sector pages only to capture search queries.
- Do not pressure partners to use a specific anchor; an accurate brand or descriptive link is sufficient.

The standard is simple: the profile must represent a real business, and an editorial link must be useful to the linking site's audience even if search engines did not exist.
