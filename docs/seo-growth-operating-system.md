## Weekly publishing cadence

Publish only genuine, owner-confirmed inventory.

### Monday
- Publish one current property listing with a unique title, full unit facts, asking price and approved images.
- Add the property to the relevant project, sector and corridor pages.
- Publish one Google Business Profile update linking directly to the canonical listing URL.

### Wednesday
- Publish one current property listing.
- Add one short property walkthrough to YouTube and embed/link it on the listing when approved.
- Share the direct listing URL through WhatsApp and relevant social channels.

### Friday
- Publish one current property listing or refresh a still-available priority listing.
- Publish one Google Business Profile update with an actual property or project image.
- Review Search Console impressions, clicks, indexed pages and leading queries.

Target: 3 verified listings each week initially. Increase to 5 only when the owner, availability, price and media can all be kept current.

- Carpet/super/built-up area with the type of area identified
- Floor and total floors
- Facing and view
- Furnishing and possession/occupancy status
- Parking
- Asking price and whether negotiable
- Owner confirmation date
- Approved photos/video
- Home-loan suitability only when not overstated
- NRI/remote viewing availability where relevant

One follow-up after 5–7 days:

A gentle reminder—if you have a moment, your honest Google review would help our Gurugram real estate advisory reach more genuine clients. Thank you again. [INSERT VERIFIED GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Response standard:
- Reply to every review individually.
- Thank the reviewer without revealing private transaction details.
- Mention the relevant service naturally when the reviewer has already made it public.
- Do not reuse the same templated response repeatedly.

## Safe citation and backlink targets

Create or correct listings only with consistent business name, office address, primary phone, website and category.

1. Google Business Profile — primary local identity.
2. Bing Places — claim the same office identity.
3. Apple Business Connect — establish the office on Apple Maps.
4. Mappls — verify the Gurugram office pin and business details.
5. Justdial — maintain one accurate business listing.
6. Facebook business page — consistent contact details and website.
7. LinkedIn company page — founder and company identity with the official domain.
8. YouTube channel — property walkthroughs linking to the exact listing pages.
9. Relevant builder/channel-partner directories — only where Shubh Estate Brokers is genuinely authorized or eligible.
10. Local business associations or professional memberships — only real memberships with verifiable profile pages.
11. Reputable Gurgaon property or business publications — contribute original market commentary, not paid link insertions.
12. Professional partners — seek relevant referral/resource links from genuine mortgage, legal, relocation or NRI-service partners.

Avoid:
- Bulk directory submissions.
- Purchased backlink packages.
- Private blog networks.
- Irrelevant guest posts.
- Exact-match anchor text repeated across many sites.
- Fake reviews or fake office locations.
